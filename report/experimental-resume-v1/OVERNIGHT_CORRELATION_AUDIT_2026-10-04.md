# Correlation evidence for the overnight routing comparison — 2026-10-04

This audit reads existing saved results and implementation; it performs no new fit, inference or Slurm submission. It ranks evidence by usefulness for selecting an intervention, rather than by the largest numerical statistic. The strongest available target identities remain the conditional Qwen3.6 native expert contrasts. The clean global readouts establish class information, but do not themselves identify which expert to amplify or suppress.

## Concrete overnight comparison recommended

1. **Recruitment versus amplification on existing correlation-selected targets.** Same qualified prefixes, balanced seeds, same 256-token pulse and 1,024-token continuation: target +1 logit bias, target force-in with native fixed-k replacement, and target selected-weight reweighting. Use native repeats and operator-matched random controls, record achieved occupancy and gate displacement. Fixed-membership reweighting tests whether amplifying already-selected experts helps, while force-in tests whether recruitment was limiting. Each operator needs its own execution qualification before the generation stage; an existing bias qualification is not transferable automatically.
2. **Decompose the Verify pair.** Test L28/E189 alone and L28/E9 alone against the pair and matched one-expert controls under one common operator. Both singletons come directly from strong saved correlations; this checks whether one useful expert was diluted by the pair or whether both are needed. Freeze this as new exploratory selection with explicit multiplicity and future independent validation.
3. **Functional reranking in parallel.** Use the prepared counterfactual token-loss qualification, then a discovery-only same-prefix target/non-target loss screen with syntax controls. Native repeats establish measurement noise. A continuation's native likelihood is not its semantic value; selection should use selective loss effects on independently accepted target continuations. The resulting functional ranking is an additional candidate source, not a reason to delay the already runnable free-generation methods.
4. **Optional timing comparison, separately scoped.** Saved accepted approach starts are early (five exact-pool starts at tokens 178–269). A prompt-start 256-token pulse is a motivated exploratory timing arm and avoids online-trigger scarcity; its original-prompt population and sequence endpoint differ from accepted-prefix mechanism effects. Do not silently pool it with the existing local estimand. A broad utility run still needs a complete price and a frozen detector/action policy.

## Weaker proposals and specific gaps

- The exact-last-64 Verify contrast selects **L29/{118,226}**, with membership differences +0.03217/+0.01993, but held-out proposal effects have positive direction in only 2/4 folds. The whole-sentence L28 signal and exact-boundary L29 signal differ: timing/feature support is a genuine uncertainty to test, not proof of either expert set's causal role.
- The persistence/loop contrast selects **L21/{225,49}**, +0.02697/+0.02476 in the oriented negative-source score, but again only 2/4 positive held-out folds; expert49 has no same-layer ±10% native-exposure peer. This is not yet a strong grounded deactivation policy. A single-expert225 screen could be a later amended exploratory candidate, but no claim of stronger support is justified.
- The semantic-only Verify shortlist at layers23–26 has just six target-positive families and one within-family matched pair; its larger raw differences are not stronger evidence. Failed-check→revision has no target-negative support. Do not expand those into an overnight unrestricted search.
- The strongest Verify selector uses the **entire completed source sentence**, and its outcome is next-sentence LLM **Verify class**, not an unchecked candidate followed by substantive verification. Four-fold statistics evaluate the proposals selected within each training fold, not independent confidence intervals for the final fixed pair. Preserve this distinction in the paper.
- Native exposure matching has not matched actual pulse dose. Positive-pilot superiority to random may reflect more recruitment as well as expert identity. Operator-matched controls and measured dose must accompany semantic estimates.
- Reader agreement is not human validation: historical dense-label agreement is 87% on 200 balanced fixtures; both are model-generated labels. Current semantic estimates use two stochastic readers from the same reader model. Report them as LLM audits.
- No reranking may use the newly completing independent mechanism outcomes while still calling that same pool untouched validation. Any new methods informed by those outcomes require a separately named exploratory comparison or fresh independent families.

## Functional-score qualification status checked during this audit

The original 148-assignment counterfactual qualification completed but failed
its raw-score/teacher-force alignment gate: maximum absolute difference
0.021752 nats, native-repeat maximum 0.011340; recovery itself passed
(job 59189255). Serial/eager execution was subsequently tried on 80 API
calibration assignments and also failed (job 59198795): cross-API B mean
absolute difference 0.000567 exceeded the repeat-calibrated 0.000445 limit;
its maximum was 0.008984 and teacher-force diagnostic maximum 0.003706.

The serial qualification already used max_num_seqs=1, enforce_eager=True and
disabled prefix caching. Native repeated requests still had unequal routed
expert sets; the largest score error was concentrated in one prefix's first
position. This shows that merely serializing again is not a new recovery
hypothesis. It does not identify whether FP8 numerical execution, near-tied
router decisions or another implementation issue causes that variability.
The next functional-selection experiment must resolve that measurement gap
with a distinct controlled execution hypothesis before treating token-loss
differences as a qualified expert ranking. Free-generation operator comparisons
can proceed independently under their own passed execution qualification.
