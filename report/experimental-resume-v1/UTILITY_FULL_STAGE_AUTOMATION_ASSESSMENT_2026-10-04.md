# Full 96-family utility continuation: implementation assessment

The user's authorization covers the workload and long allocations. No further permission request is needed solely because the measured cost exceeds the original exploratory budget. Engineering qualification, frozen policy selection, complete-stage pricing and exact submission verification remain execution gates. No full-study job has been submitted by this agent.

## What already exists

- `UTILITY_SCOUT_PLAN_v1.json` fixes 96 families, two arms, two seeds and 384 canonical assignment UIDs, with a 16,384-token endpoint. The runtime pilot covers the first two families and eight of these exact UIDs.
- `utility_pair_backend_v2.py` implements the original-prompt generator and current-prefix semantic reader on four GPUs. Its qualified scientific source closure contains 208 files and must remain unchanged. The policy selector, token observer, sampler and first-accepted-episode rule are already implemented.
- Pilot receipts contain generation wall time, all emitted tokens, every semantic query and its latency/token counts, nonfires, closure/cap status, routing arrays, attempts and source bindings. `PAIR_LOAD.json` records joint load time; `PAIR_COST.json` records driver wall time. Slurm elapsed time supplies the authoritative four-GPU allocation cost, including waits and shutdown. These measurements must not be added twice.
- The v4 one-shot attachment handles Slurm jobs that have aged out of dependency records: a completed prerequisite requires `sacct COMPLETED 0:0` plus a matching sealed result. Active prerequisites retain their `afterok` edges.

## Exact remaining production work

1. **Measured profile and full-stage price builder.** Consume the sealed pilot manifest, every attempt/receipt, pair load/cost records and final Slurm accounting. Report generator-only and side-reader time separately for diagnosis, but charge the whole four-GPU allocation once. Include side queries from vetoes and nonfires; include failed loads/attempts and all cold loads in proposed shards. Price final answer grading and any specified dense semantic measurement separately. Record an empirical forecast, a stated stress forecast and the requested allocation ceiling as different quantities. Eight assignments cannot establish a statistical upper bound for all 384 assignments.
2. **Versioned production manifest and shard generator.** Keep the unchanged 384-assignment parent plan. Import clean pilot receipts by exact UID, policy, prompt, sampler, source and execution-profile hashes; preserve their original receipt and allocation provenance. Partition only the remaining assignments, keeping all four cells of a family together where possible, and prove exact disjoint coverage before submission. The old `run_utility_scout_v1.py` is a stub and its execution schema does not accept the implemented v2 policy/qualification. A new production entry point is required; merely setting `pilot_families=96` would not implement parallel shards or safe pilot reuse.
3. **Bounded execution and recovery.** Reuse the frozen backend through an entry point that bootstraps the qualified namespace before any scientific import. Each shard needs its own writer lock, manifest binding, durable assignment/attempt journal, atomic route/result commits and deadline handling. A timeout must leave incomplete cells visible. A later allocation can execute only unresolved assignments after exact journal reconciliation. The current pilot can restart an uncommitted assignment from its original prompt; it does not checkpoint and resume partial token generation. Reusing partial generation would require a separate replay qualification. Do not silently retry committed failed outcomes until they succeed. Any infrastructure retry policy must be frozen, preserve the initial attempt and charge every retry.
4. **One-shot Slurm chain.** After pilot accounting, prepare the measured complete-stage proposal on CPU. Recheck qualification, policy/source hashes and current partition/account limits, then submit the fully priced GPU array with durable attempted/submitted/verified receipts. A final CPU task after all terminal shard jobs reconciles Slurm states, exit codes, exact UID coverage and actual cost. It must report incomplete cells without selecting or adding families. A finite recovery manifest can be prepared from infrastructure failures; no daemon or unbounded automatic retry loop is required.
5. **Outcome measurement and ITT analysis.** A utility-specific grading adapter is still needed. Reuse the established `moe_steer.score` strict/J1 machinery after validating its exact frozen rubric/model contract; the M9 wrapper itself is tied to M9 and cannot be used unchanged. Materialize gold/reference answers only in offline blinded grading. Keep all 384 assigned cells, including capped answers, nonfires, early finishes, errors and missing results. Freeze the treatment of capped but answer-bearing outputs and missing grades before viewing utility accuracy. Produce family-clustered simultaneous accuracy/token intervals and their joint region, with the fixed two seeds averaged within family; report side-reader token and GPU cost alongside generator-token effects. The original utility contract requests these intervals but no complete v2 utility analyzer currently exists.

## Resource and scientific limits

The current qualification's observed generator rate is about 11.4 tokens/s on a sequential request. Extrapolating that rate to the 6,291,456-token maximum gives about 153 generator wall-hours, or 613 four-GPU hours, before extra side-reader time and repeated loads. This is a rough cap-length projection from qualification, not a measured full-stage price; natural stops can reduce it substantially. It explains why the old 4.75-GPU-hour utility line must not be reused as an execution estimate.

Uncapped semantic-query count remains part of the fixed policy. Pilot-driven sharding can reduce elapsed completion time, but cannot turn the observed mean query count into a worst-case guarantee. A bounded allocation may end incomplete and need a separately recorded continuation. Hardware concurrency and account limits determine how many four-GPU shards can run together; no specific throughput or tomorrow-morning completion is established before the pilot and live scheduling checks.

The proposed implementation changes orchestration, manifests and measurement adapters around the qualified generator. It does not change the selected actions, family enrollment, trigger rule, pulse geometry or 16k endpoint. A successful runtime pilot establishes execution and a pricing reference; it does not establish steering benefit.
