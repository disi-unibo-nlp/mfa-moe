\section{Immediate Work}

The remaining priorities are independent assessment of generated-trace
labels; external-domain probe validation with thresholds and abstention
rules selected inside training folds; matched problem cohorts and annotation
protocols across models; and
length-/termination-aware correlation models evaluated on held-out problems.
The offline equivalence audit is complete for all saved baseline and
guided evaluations. Further scoring validation should adjudicate residual
parser failures and ambiguous structured answers and assess sensitivity
to extraction and tolerance choices.
Routing intervention intervals resample source-problem clusters. OSS
negative-lift controls and four-target policies are complete; remaining
controls concern random expert targets, smaller target budgets, calibration
sensitivity, independent decoding seeds, and transfer across quantizations. Strength selection and final
evaluation require separate problem sets. Class-conditioned routing remains
unevaluated.
