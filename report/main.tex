\section{Immediate Work}

The agreed order of work is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item freeze and archive the seed prompt retained by the completed final GEPA
alignment, and reserve the locked test for a prespecified evaluation;
\item independently assess sentence labels, document token-limit sensitivity,
and extend coverage to the missing benchmarks;
\item fit a prespecified length-/termination-aware, dataset-aware correctness
model with problem-respecting validation and exploratory layerwise screening;
\item validate the completed repeated-problem analyses, test class/position
contrasts on common cohorts, and qualify
pooled expert-ID candidates by layer; and
\item extend the completed Qwen, Gemma, and GPT-OSS comparison with matched
annotation coverage and held-out validation.
\end{enumerate}
The detailed acceptance criteria and transcript ambiguities are maintained in
\texttt{NEXT\_STEPS.md} at the repository root.
