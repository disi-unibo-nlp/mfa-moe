\section{Immediate Work}

The remaining priorities are independent assessment of generated-trace
labels; response-grouped probe validation with layer selection inside the
training folds; matched annotation coverage across models; and
length-/termination-aware correlation models evaluated on held-out problems.
For routing interventions, uncertainty should respect source-problem clusters,
and further controls should assess random expert targets, calibration
sensitivity, and transfer across quantizations. Strength selection and final
evaluation require separate problem sets. Class-conditioned routing remains
unevaluated.
