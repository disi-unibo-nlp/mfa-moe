# GPT 6.1 Sol xhigh counterargument to DeepSeek round 1

Reviewer: user-requested parallel GPT-6.1 Sol at xhigh. Source-grounded read-only review; no experiment submission. Recorded 2026-10-07.

I agree with the distinctions between prediction, execution qualification, semantic effects and utility, and with preserving missingness and scoped uncertainty. I disagree with several factual and decision-rule premises.

1. The .852/.780/.788 probe is a HIDDEN-STATE prefix probe, not a routing/state-feature experiment. probeTest.tex says it encodes the prefix before the upcoming SAT episode. It already uses response-disjoint outer folds, inner layer selection and response-bootstrap intervals. Re-running response clustering is not an unresolved repair. Richer nuisance controls can add evidence, but Stage 0 should not recover validity already established.
2. It is not the only positive result. The saved readout-results.v3.json reports seven-model CLEAN routing readout gains over token and retrospective judge-context baselines; all seven judge-context simultaneous CI7 intervals exclude zero. The current claim ledger supersedes old incomplete-cell status. anticipation-results.json shows GPT next-class loss change -.02073 [-.02548,-.01601] over 509 questions/10209 pairs and Qwen3.6 -.05570 [-.06944,-.04154] over 494/2492. These are ROUTING results and core positive evidence.
3. A '70% discovery/held-out standard' invents a gate. The preserved gate is ten eligible discovery families and >=70% APPARENT precision, with disjoint held-out uncertainty/coverage and symbolic audit. A held-out 70% threshold was expressly not adopted (final/REPORT.md). Criticizing imprecision does not authorize changing this criterion.
4. Same-hidden-state fidelity/dose differs from cross-arm comparability. Within an execution, saved native and executed routes are compared at the same hidden state. Native and treated free continuations diverge after intervention. Matched-prefix/paired-seed arms need exact prefix/contracts and valid assignment/outcome inference, not identical hidden states or routes throughout separate continuations. Native repeat variability is disclosed engineering noise; requiring arms to share one execution is not the current contract.
5. The token endpoint is GENERATOR + CONTROLLER OUTPUT tokens. Processed input, runtime, annotation/grading and bounded research recovery are separately itemized resource expenditures. Adding prompt tokens silently changes the estimand.
6. Positive causal control does not require a positive accuracy interval. Increasing the prespecified substantive transition under a valid comparison can demonstrate control with uncertain or adverse accuracy/cost. Usefulness is separate. Preserve the frozen validation rule rather than an invented accuracy-significance gate.
7. Serialization repair cannot solve the frozen proposal support of 0/0/4 among 48 apparent prefixes. A corrected reader can measure precision and reader availability but cannot qualify all six unchanged decisions. Feasibility comes before another GPU proposal.
8. The user REQUIRES ALL SEVEN analyzed models, including causal verification, with verification first. Staged execution cannot narrow the scientific scope. Latest cross-4/F_summary.md already includes seven A and seven B roots, superseding Sept28 three-A coverage. Use a common verification protocol with model-specific identities, native k and qualified executors. Do not replace this with a one-model measurement paper.
