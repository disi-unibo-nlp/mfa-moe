## How to keep one research direction

Before proposing work, cite the relevant [catalogue entries](EXPERIMENT_INVENTORY.md#r04-requirement-for-any-future-computation-proposal) and exact existing artifacts, then establish what required information they cannot supply. Distinguish unavailable files, uninspected compatibility and unmeasured outcomes. Inspect the sources first. A proposal must say what existing result it builds on, which claim it can change, and what will happen if it fails. Update the relevant page and decision record; do not create another competing top-level plan.

This wiki consolidates the major preserved result branches and their final receipts. It does not claim to have rerun every raw tensor analysis. Archived table-backed results and directly inspected result artifacts are distinguished in [Sources](SOURCES.md). Original sources remain unchanged. A newer document supersedes an older status only for the same population, endpoint, implementation, and scope.
