\section{Preserved Verification Evidence and Protocol}
\label{sec:verification-study}

This appendix retains the verification study's original observation
snapshot and frozen protocol. Pending-stage statements below describe
that snapshot, not current scheduler ownership. The later seven-model
campaign has not established a qualified joint accuracy--memory result;
engineering completion and failed allocations are not scientific effects.

The paper separates information in native routing, the ability to change
routing, the effect on reasoning operations, and the resulting utility.
The saved CLEAN readout improves label-prediction cross-entropy over a
retrospective judge-context baseline in all seven checkpoints
(Table~\ref{tab:seven-readout-current}). Its simultaneous intervals concern
label readout; they do not establish that promoting a correlated expert
causes the corresponding reasoning operation.

Historical next-class anticipation is already measured for GPT-OSS and
Qwen3.6. Their routing loss changes are $-0.02073$
($[-0.02548,-0.01601]$) and $-0.05570$ ($[-0.06944,-0.04154]$), respectively,
under the saved past-information controls and family-grouped analysis.
Equivalent prepared token-window caches are unavailable for the other
five checkpoints. Their positive native readout results remain usable;
an anticipation claim for all seven would need the missing cells.
The historical FRESH verification contrast against native is
$+5.5$ percentage points, with simultaneous 95\% interval
$[-4.5,+15.5]$. It establishes neither improvement nor a precise absence
of control. The completed dense ledger contains 187,524 identities:
187,136 valid, 388 unusable and none unresolved.

The prospective method uses one discovery-nominated singleton expert per
checkpoint. Family/source-trace matched checked-versus-unchecked profiles
use only the last 256 already-emitted prediction rows. This nomination is
a destination-state association heuristic; checked windows can contain
the verification itself. It does not establish anticipation or exact
position matching. The causal comparison determines actionability.

The action adds a positive bias to the expert's raw routing logit and
calls the architecture's own selector. Native top-$k$, shared experts,
grouped/sigmoid rules, normalization and learned scales are preserved.
One pulse lasts at most 256 prediction rows and stops at operational
native reasoning closure. Discovery-only same-state calibration targets
an inclusion increment of $0.25\pm0.025$. Random controls exclude the
nominee and match native exposure, calibrated dose and ID-aligned relative
weight displacement. No admissible pool is a qualification failure.

Candidate recognition includes intermediate/complete numeric or symbolic
values checkable against an original problem constraint. It distinguishes
a candidate-specific substantive check from intention or checking another
value. Two draws from a fixed reader audit eligible and already-checked
proposals. Qualification requires at least ten apparent discovery families
and 70\% apparent family precision. Disjoint held-out uncertainty, coverage
and symbolic cases are separate; the reader is not human ground truth.
Before the first recognizer or target was selected, we amended decoding
to accept either a plain JSON object or one complete outer \texttt{json}
code fence around the final response. The amendment applies to all seven
models and preserves the original draws; truncated or ambiguous outputs
remain unconfirmed.

The experiments have different populations. The conditional assay freezes
the first qualified native seed 0 prefix on each of 96 independent families,
without seed 1 fallback, before any treatment. Fresh native, targeted and
matched-random continuations use two seeds and a 1,024-token cap. The event
is verification before native closure and within that horizon. Closure
competes with verification; all assigned branches remain in the analysis.
The original-prompt experiment uses all 96 families, two seeds, three arms
and a 16,384-token cap. It retains no-fire/failed assignments and reuses only
exact compatible original native sources. Maximum final enrollment is
4,032 long runs plus 4,032 short continuations across seven checkpoints;
discovery and validation costs are separate.

All seven checkpoints passed the 14 backend qualification checks under
the current executor. One retained native discovery trace per checkpoint
has also completed with validated routing captures. These traces belong
to the frozen 48-family discovery manifests and are reused there.
In the current execution snapshot, GPT-OSS, Nemotron, Gemma, Qwen3-30B and Qwen3.6
have completed all 48 discovery traces, each producing a 128-prefix audit.
Qwen3.5 and GLM remain in discovery; Qwen3.6 has begun its retained
largest-input reader measurement before the full semantic audit.
The status table reports recognizer and calibrated-control qualification
separately from causal and utility outcomes.

GPT-OSS has completed all 256 fixed eligibility-reader draws. Under the
declared decoding amendment, 196 provide usable stopped judgments, 36 are
truncated and 24 fail the original content validator; 84 of the 128
prefixes have two usable draws. Apparent family-weighted confirmation
precision is 22.41\% for eligibility across 29 families and 18.75\% for
already-checked proposals across eight families. Both fall below the
70\% gate, and already-checked support also falls below ten families.
GPT therefore stops before target nomination or treatment. This is a
qualification limit of the current controller; GPT's causal effect and
utility remain unmeasured. The other checkpoints retain their own gates.

Nemotron's complete 256-draw audit also fails recognition qualification.
It provides 191 usable stopped judgments, 37 truncated draws and 28
content-invalid draws; 81 prefixes have two usable judgments. Apparent
family-weighted confirmation is 25\% for eligibility across 34 families
and 10\% for already-checked proposals across ten families. Both decisions
meet the apparent support requirement but fail the 70\% precision gate.
Normally stopped agreeing readers also reject proposals, so the failure
is not attributable solely to output truncation. Nemotron stops before
nomination or treatment; its causal and utility outcomes remain unmeasured.
Qwen3-30B's full audit likewise fails recognition qualification. Of its
256 draws, 193 provide usable stopped judgments, 39 are truncated and 24
fail content validation; 80 prefixes have two usable judgments. Apparent
family-weighted confirmation is 10.10\% for eligibility across 33 families
and 33.33\% for already-checked proposals across 12 families. Both support
minima pass and both precision gates fail. Agreeing stopped readers reject
22 apparent eligibility proposals and five already-checked proposals.
This model also stops before nomination or treatment, leaving its causal
and utility outcomes unmeasured.

Gemma's complete 256-draw audit also fails recognition qualification.
It provides 209 usable stopped judgments, 30 truncated draws and 17
content-invalid draws; 91 prefixes have two usable judgments. Apparent
family-weighted confirmation is 25.52\% for eligibility across 32 families
and 41.67\% for already-checked proposals across 12. Both apparent support
minima pass and both precision gates fail. Agreeing stopped readers reject
14 apparent eligibility proposals and two already-checked proposals.
Gemma stops before nomination or treatment, with causal and utility outcomes
unmeasured. Qwen3.5, Qwen3.6 and GLM continue through their own gates.

Accuracy is graded offline, with gold absent from the generator and
semantic reader. The token endpoint is generator plus online-controller
output tokens. Input processing, runtime, annotation/grading, capture and
research recovery are itemized separately. Unknown outcomes retain
worst-case bounds. Seeds are averaged within family before shared family
resampling across checkpoints; 42 contrasts receive simultaneous intervals.
The shared equivalence grader matched 97 of 100 frozen agent-consensus
labels and made no false equivalence judgments on clear-wrong controls.
It failed qualification because all three disagreements occurred in the
Minerva stratum, exceeding the prespecified limit of two. All nine draws
for those cases stopped normally and produced valid JSON; the disagreements
concerned contextual qualifications or interpretation of the reference.
These consensus labels are not independent human ground truth. The failed
grader is excluded from production adjudication. Strictly accepted answers
and known capped or absent-final-answer failures retain their grades;
uncapped strict rejects remain unknown and enter the accuracy bounds.
The separate semantic reader and token endpoint retain their own contracts.
No accuracy-loss margin has been chosen, so nonsignificant loss cannot
establish retention. Backend PASS qualifies execution, not a semantic
effect. A valid scoped limit, harm or tradeoff is a possible paper result.
